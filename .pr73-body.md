## Long Live — the era/threads Vault reader

A full front-end experience for the Swift2 Vault: an era-themed, scroll-driven reader with a parallel narrative-threads mode. This layer is **self-contained and statically-authored** (`apps/web/components/longlive/**` + `apps/web/lib/longlive/**`); it does not yet read from the Supabase two-tier Vault path and is documented to converge with it later.

**Read `docs/longlive-experience.md` before touching this layer** — it is the source of truth (data model, store, theming, feature surfaces, add-content recipes, and the consistency invariants). Cross-linked from `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/architecture.md`.

### What's in here

**Core reader**
- Two modes: **Era** (vertical, color-morphing era stream with a timeline scrubber) and **Threads** (narrative lenses across her career).
- Per-era theming: each era re-skins the whole UI via `--era-*` tokens (never hardcoded).

**Feature surfaces**
- **Era Spotify embeds** (`EraMedia`) — click-to-play album facades.
- **YouTube music-video embeds** (`MomentVideo`) on 10 signature moments — click-to-play, privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com`, no re-hosting. All video IDs verified against YouTube oEmbed (caught + fixed 2 bad IDs).
- **Clue Web mini-app** — guided motif trails (plant→payoff cards, confirmed/fan-theory flags, sources) plus an opt-in constellation "explore" mode.
- **Eras × Threads pivot + Crossings overlay** — jump sideways from an era into any thread running through it and back; lay any two threads over one shared career axis to find where her stories intersect.
- **Threads**: "The Decode" (hidden-clues) and "The Proposal".

**Navigation polish**
- **Era-stream scroll restoration**: toggling to Threads and back drops you exactly where you were (anchor era, appended eras, scroll offset). Explicit jumps still land at the top. See invariant §5.6 in the manual — *any new jump path must call `clearEraScroll()`*.

**Cleanups**
- Removed the redundant "Back to [era]" transition band (now a pure color-morph gradient).
- Fixed the Life of a Showgirl hero (was rendering intro prose instead of a lyric).
- Removed song-title credits under era quotes.
