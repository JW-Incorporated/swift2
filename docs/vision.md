# Vision

Owner: Joey. Status: v0.1 — high level, to be refined as we learn.



The user experience we're after is a unique one for Taylor Swift fans. Primarily the app is a "recent news" app that uses AI \& user input to ensure stories are real, filters out fake stories (or possibly presents the fake stories but ensures the fans know they are fake). The "recent news" extends to literally every aspect of Taylor's life; music, fashion, travel, tours, relationship, business, etc. One of the best parts of the app is that user notifications are presented as an integral part of the experience, not some settings screen you change. The user MUST get notified only for what they want to know about, at the rate they prefer. We never want to over-notify someone. We want to bring people back to the app by only feeding them extremely high quality notifications at the rate they want them. The secondary part of the app is where the website architecture is probably most critical, and where the user interaction is most unique. Here, the user can go back in time. They get the same experience as the "recent news" section of the app, except  at a different point in time, divided by Taylor's "eras". So a user selects an era and instantly is transported to that era via UI updates to colors, fonts, and design. They also have a timeline slider (or some UI experience) where they can go to any month in that era and look through the news of that point in time, where Taylor traveled, where she was spotted, what her fashion choices were at the time, who she was dating, what was going on in her life, current tours, current songs being written, etc. This is a very high level vision that will have to be refined. 





* What is the app, in two sentences? Awesome recent news + awesome time travel experience 
* Who is it for, and what moment in a Swiftie's life does it serve? It's for all swifties, to stay updated
* Why will people love it more than what already exists? I hope so. That's the idea
* How does it make money? No clue
* What is explicitly OUT of scope for v1? Monetization
