## Judgment — is the plan on track?

**Three of the four things this plan set out to build are working. The fourth —
earned autonomy — is further away than it was two weeks ago, and not for a
reason the plan anticipated.**

What is genuinely working: the gate (every ledger row carries
`approver: discord:338508192755482626`; nothing posted unapproved in four
weeks), latency (median under 3.5h for three of four weeks), the reason
protocol (6 reasons in W40 alone, verbatim), two-way replies (3 of 4 weeks),
and Tree owning strategy outright — #5871 is Tree writing a measurable target
into the strategy doc and landing it, which is exactly T1's goal state.

### The one finding that matters

**T7 is unreachable by arithmetic, not by quality.** Eligibility needs 8 briefs
in a rolling 28 days with zero rejections. At 2–4 posts a week, a *rolling*
28-day window drains faster than briefs arrive, so a family that sat at 8/8 in
W39 is at 2 today without a single new rejection. Shop-the-look did not get
worse; it got **quieter**. The 0-rejection clause was always the hard bar — but
the brief-count clause is now the binding one, and it is a supply problem:
the calendar ran dry on 10-15/10-16 (#5895, OPEN — `.scratch/tree-inputs.json`
reports `needsDraft: false`, `filled: []`, backlog 0).

That is why **Wave 5 must not start.** Its entry gate asks R4 for a family at
or within two briefs of eligibility. The honest answer is no, by a margin of
four briefs on the best family — and the eligibility corpus is still
contaminated by 9 null pillars, so even that answer is UNVERIFIABLE. Building
signed grants now would ship the riskiest code in the repo against a bar
nothing can clear.
