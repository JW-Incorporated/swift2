## Judgment — is the plan on track?

**Three of this plan's four goals are met and holding. The fourth was dissolved
by a founder decision seven days before R4 ran, and neither R4 nor anyone else
noticed.**

Holding: the gate (every post since 09-20 carries a stamp — 60 stamps, none
missing), brief delivery (4 of 4 on schedule, the guard-veto path removed),
Tree owning strategy outright, and supply (recovered from 2 to 5–6 briefs a
week, 503 never-used photos banked, a draft PR opened today).

Dissolved: **earned autonomy.** T7 was built to grant per-family lighter review
on a measured record of plain ✅s. On 2026-10-09 the founder granted blanket
self-clearance for IG/X instead. The ladder now measures a signal that is no
longer produced — which is why every family reads 0–2 briefs while posting runs
at its highest rate in six weeks. R4 read the same collapse and diagnosed
*supply*; supply has since recovered and the ladder numbers did **not**, which
settles it: the cause is the approval-regime change, not volume.

This is why Wave 5 must not be built, on a stronger ground than R4's. R4 said
"the bar is unmet." R5 says **the bar is unmeetable, and meeting it would
require rolling back a founder decision.** Wave 5's deliverables — signed
grants, approval schema `v: 3`, policy stamping, posted-under-policy notices —
would re-implement, with more code and more risk, a narrower version of what
`social-tree-approve.yml` already does in 251 lines with committed-identity and
`SOCIAL_FREEZE` checks. The X-delete proof and the fresh irrevocable-Instagram
re-confirmation in its entry gate are moot; retire the wave.
