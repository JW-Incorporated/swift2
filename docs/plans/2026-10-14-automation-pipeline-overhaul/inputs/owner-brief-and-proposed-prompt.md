"Fix all of it and satisfy all our goals, agentically, fully automated, with minimal and smart token use. Sonnet and haiku have both gotten much smarter recently, so lean into them wherever possible. There's an idealized automation flow: a bot that checks every content pipeline once an hour (or so), looks for new things vs old things (how do we track what we already have?), and classifies them using Jev. Jev is an excellent, cheap tool for deciding when something is, for example, 'critical breaking news', as long as it gets enough context. We need to brainstorm the classifications. Certainly one is 'breaking news'. Think about notifications too.

Depending on Jev's classification we take action. When Jev is pretty certain, flow immediately into the right branch. When Jev isn't sure, have the latest Haiku classify instead and trust its judgment. Big breaking news gets fact-checked, then posted immediately to the site, social, and users with that notification enabled. Normal news also gets posted. Other stuff flows normally, and we need to define what 'normally' means. Duplicate content gets discarded, but first we gather comments and look for Easter-egg theories. Merch is handled similarly.

Consider where Jev can help in decision-making."
