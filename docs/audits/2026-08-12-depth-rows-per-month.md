## Analysis — what the numbers say (hand-written, not generated)

1. **The launch bar's hard floor holds.** Every wavetop (milestone) month in
   all 12 eras carries at least one row — the 2026-07-04 floor from issue #38
   still stands. Nobody knew this until today; now it's evidenced.
2. **One flagship deficit: Tortured Poets' `relationship` axis (2 rows era-wide
   vs a floor of 4).** Exactly the axis the 2026-07-04 launch bar weighted and
   the axis `docs/launch-readiness.md` flagged as the corpus's weakest. The
   era leans on 9 `sighting` rows (Chiefs games, dinners) where relationship
   *milestones* should also exist — candidates in the window include the
   couple's first Coachella outing (April 2024). Queue: 2 sourced
   relationship rows.
3. **1989 is the deepest month-grid hole.** Lowest rows/month of any era
   (1.47), 17 zero months, and a near-dead 14-month stretch (2016-10 →
   2017-11, 1 row total) that contains a genuinely era-defining, fully
   sourceable event the corpus does not carry at all: **the August 2017
   David Mueller trial** (the corpus's only mentions are one passing clause
   in a Midnights item and a candidates file). On top of that, 7 of the
   era's headline items are captions under 300 chars ("The pop reinvention",
   "Shake It Off launches the era", "Blank Space flips the narrative"…).
4. **Caption-level marquee items cluster in the four oldest walked eras** —
   red (7), 1989 (7), speak-now (6), evermore (6). These are the
   highest-leverage thin *pages* (as opposed to thin months): they're the
   era-headline moments a fan lands on first.
5. **Pacing flags are mostly benign** — release-month pileups (2020-12: 24
   rows for evermore) already ticketed as #696 (P3), plus the current era's
   news lane. Report-only, no action queued here.
6. **Cross-reference to Karen's 2026-08-12 run** (`docs/audits/engine/`):
   `content.depth-deficit` (defining items under the 4-axis floor) = 22,
   `content.photo-sparsity` = 59. Those queues are item-level; this audit
   adds the month axis neither checker sees.
