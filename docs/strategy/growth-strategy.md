## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** social drives to the site. We post one genuinely shareable thing every day where fans already are (Instagram and X, paired), take part in fan communities as real members, lead with Taylor's own videos the day they drop, and give fans a card they want to post themselves; the site is where they land.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-10-18):** flat. 41 visitors this week against 42 the week before, and most of it is still not fans — the privacy page took 25 of 63 pageviews and 56 of 63 came "direct". Followers did not move (Instagram 4, X 2, Facebook 11). Only 2 post pairs went out: the account was dark 10-12 to 10-15 because the Claude account hit its weekly usage limit, and from 10-15 every new draft was refused by a broken photo check (fixed 2026-10-18, [#6077](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6077)). The site side is strong: 9 of 11 real-world moments live within 48 hours, often within an hour.
- **Order of work:** (1) the posting engine runs all seven days — both of this week's breakages are fixed, now it has to prove it, (2) Taylor's official videos land in the videos rail the day they drop (Patient Zero still missing; visualizers have no slot yet), (3) the site's same-day news also goes out on social (1 of 13 this week), (4) the awareness replies get posted (0 of 56 leads in two weeks), (5) share cards as a loop. Growth is priority #1.
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever once traffic is meaningful; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors, from 41 now to 200 by 2026-12-31 (lowered from 500 — three flat weeks with the engine never running a full week). Leading indicator: Instagram followers 25 by 2026-11-15 and 150 by 2026-12-31 (moved from 2026-11-30); 500 moves to 2027-03-31.

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties of every age who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory-and-easter-egg readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see what happened in a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified (the "recent news" half of `docs/vision.md`); the time-travel by era (the other half); Taylor's own videos; fashion ("shop the look"); something worth sharing.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit (r/TaylorSwift and adjacent), Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1). Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come before any promo post.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** their age mix, how many are app-first versus web, and which format earns shares. Instagram reach per post is 1 or 2 accounts — at that size no format comparison is readable yet.
- **Traffic reality:** 41 visitors, nearly all "direct"; the privacy page (25 of 63 pageviews) and support page (7) look like app-review or automated traffic, not fans. Social-tagged links brought 2 pageviews this week (12 the week before, when more posts went out). No channel sends fans at volume yet; the only fan-shaped referrers were Bing (2) and Facebook (1).

## How we grow (bets, ranked, each with the metric that proves/kills it)

1. **Daily paired posts on Instagram and X (Tree drafts and clears his own drafts via the signed `tree-auto` workflow — guardrail 1 as amended 2026-10-09; the owner's ✅ remains only as a fallback).** Distribution is the engine; it produced 4 posts (2 pairs) this week against a plan of 14.
   - Proves it: at least 5 pairs live per week for two straight weeks, and social-tagged visits rising in `social.byCampaign` (2 pageviews this week, 12 last week).
   - Kills or reshapes it: 14 days of daily posting with fewer than 5 social-sourced visits a week means the format is wrong, not the cadence — change formats before posting more. The 14-day clock has not started: the engine has never run a full week.
   - Now: two different breakages took this week. The Claude weekly usage limit killed every Tree run 10-12 to 10-14 (now handled: [#5825](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5825) pauses non-content routines first and keeps Tree's drafting alive; the money question of buying more capacity is open with the owner as [#5873](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5873)). Then the new never-repeat-a-photo check refused every draft from 10-15 because its CI job never installed the image library (fixed 2026-10-18, [#6077](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6077)). A third, smaller break: Tree's inbox clean-up PRs fail the content build on a stale list entry ([#6081](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6081)).
2. **First and right on time-sensitive fan moments — and Taylor's own videos lead.** Fans come back daily to the place that covered the thing everyone is arguing about, accurately and within hours. Moved up from 4th: it is the one lane that already works on the site, and the cheapest growth is getting what the site already has onto social.
   - **Official Taylor videos lead the feed (owner direction 2026-10-11).** When an official video drops, the coverage IS the video: a videos entry (`supabase/seed/videos/`, synced to the reader by `scripts/sync-longlive-videos.mjs`) plus the embed on the covering moment land the same day, so the drop tops the newest-first era feed and sits under the Videos chip. This week it failed twice: the Patient Zero video is still missing a week after the direction ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)), and the official Opalite (BUNT. remix) visualizer of 10-15 could only be cited as a text source because the videos list has no visualizer kind ([#6082](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6082)).
   - Proves it: time-sensitive events on the site within 48 hours — this week 9 of 11 resolved events (two still inside the window), six within 1 hour of intake, two late (a fence-permit story at 76 hours, a Billboard 200 TV segment at 50) — and on social within hours: 1 of 13 this week, the number to move. For video drops: every official drop in the videos rail within 48 hours (0 of 2 this week).
   - Kills or reshapes it: news posts that draw less engagement than evergreen ones for three weeks running (unreadable so far — reach is 1 to 2 per post either way).
3. **The reach lane: awareness image replies first, then comments, follows, and Reddit/Facebook-group participation (the owner posts every reply; Tree finds the threads and writes every word).** Near-zero traction is a symptom of this engine never running.
   - **Awareness image replies (owner direction 2026-10-01, built as the awareness lane in `docs/community/README.md`):** Tree delivers 10+ Reddit/Facebook threads a day to Discord, each with a picture of the site (a share card for the moment or era under discussion) attached and a short reply written as if the reader had seen the site before. No link and no pitch: the goal is the reply "what is that?!", and then the owner explains.
   - Metric for this lane: opportunities delivered 17 this week (2.4 a day — down from 5.6, against the 10+ target), owner-posted 0 of 17 (0 of 56 across two measured weeks), and "what is that?" replies unmeasured (none reported in `#longlive-marjorie`). Two problems now: delivery fell, and the owner rejected a thread on 10-06 he could not reply to — leads he cannot use erode the queue, so unrepliable threads are being held back ([#5878](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5878)). Moved down from 2nd: it cannot run without the owner's hands, and it has not for four weeks.
   - Kills or reshapes this lane: two weeks with 10+ delivered a day and fewer than 3 "what is that?" replies means the picture or the thread type is wrong; change which card and which thread types, not the volume. Two more weeks with 0 posted and the lane goes to the owner as a plain keep-or-pause choice — it is his direction, so only he stops it.
   - Proves it: Reddit non-promo contributions reach 20; Instagram followers reach 25 by 2026-11-15.
4. **Share cards: fans do the distribution.** "Share as image" on an era or moment and the personal "My Eras" card (W9, `/api/share-card`, [#4668](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4668)); Tree may also post them as a sanctioned image kind.
   - Proves it: card-originated visits and logged shares rising week over week once the traffic exists (assumption: any measurable share in the first month is a win at this size). None logged to date; not re-measured this week.
   - Kills it: four weeks with zero logged shares — stop promoting cards and fold the effort into bet 2.
5. **App-store launch week (later; gated).** A realistic bump is +200 to +500 Instagram followers in 7 days, but only on a base of 150 or more (`docs/marketing/social-strategy.md` §3). Not started until bets 1 and 3 have built that base.
   - Proves it: a 7-day follower jump of that size. Kills it: launching on a sub-100 base — postpone instead.

Not a growth bet yet: **fashion monetisation.** There is nothing to monetise at 41 visitors a week; "shop the look" and the dead-link cleanup ([#4643](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4643), merged) protect trust until traffic exists. Affiliate work stays held for counsel sign-off (HUMAN-ACTIONS #27).

## Content strategy

- **The craft is top tier:** this week's moments were sourced to several outlets, honest about what could not be read (a gated Variety page did not get to set the song count; a disputed gown colour was left out), and live within an hour of intake for the biggest one — the first live "The Fate of Ophelia" ([#6067](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6067)). That honesty is the brand; never trade it for speed.
- **The gap is distribution and video, not accuracy:** the site covered 12 of 13 events, social covered 1. And the owner's video rule is not yet met: two official videos (Patient Zero, the Opalite visualizer) are not in the videos rail.
- **Photos first — for images.** Real Taylor photography is the default image ("the Taylor-photo standard"); a photo that has ever shipped is rejected — now enforced by an image fingerprint check ([#6017](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6017)) — so a deep library of never-used photos is the scarce resource. Tree's target is at least 50 new never-used photos a week while the library is under 3,000, with supply reported in every weekly plan ([#5871](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5871)). Uncredited photos are fine; credit the photographer whenever known.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** The day Taylor releases an official video — a music video, lyric video, visualizer, or official performance upload — the site leads with the video itself: a videos entry and the click-to-play embed on the covering moment (official first-party player only, per the reader's standing rule) land the same day, dated so the drop tops the newest-first feed. On Instagram and X the drop still ships as an image-plus-text pair — guardrail 5 permits no video posting — written to send fans to the video on the site.
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month (all six threads, one paired beat per thread window), the weekly Blank Spaces timeline (confirmed-only), the monthly Mood beat (deep link `/?mode=mood`, [#5594](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5594)), the daily heartbeat, and the same-day news beat. One idea goes to Instagram and X together, and a beat is drafted by its own day's run — never parked more than 24h out.
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md` (a signed approval stamp before anything posts — Tree's own `tree-auto` stamp since 2026-10-09, the owner's ✅ as fallback — rights, no AI images of Taylor, confirmed-only sensitive topics, platform limits including no video posts on IG/X, replies and DMs human for now).

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
