## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** fans have to be able to find us. The site had all 16 of this week's real-world moments up within 48 hours, most within an hour — but almost nobody sees it, and from 9 November our social accounts went silent.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-11-15):** not growing. 29 visitors against 26, 10 of them on the privacy page (app review, not fans); the home page had 17. 2 posts against 12. Followers flat a seventh week (Instagram 4, X 1, Facebook 11).
- **Why we are stuck:** a new voice judge (8 November) rejected every draft and nothing sent them back for a rewrite, and the next posting calendar never merged. Underneath, the engineering fleet cannot take most growth tickets ([#6690](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6690)), and three owner-only steps wait on him.
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors, from 29 to 100 by 2026-12-31 (serious risk), checkpoint 40 on 2026-11-30. Leading indicators: links from our posts 40 pageviews a week by 2026-11-30 (16 traced now); search 25 a week by 2026-12-31 (5 now); Instagram followers 10 by 2026-12-31 (a weak proxy).

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified; the time-travel by era (`docs/vision.md`); Taylor's own videos; fashion; something worth sharing.
- **What they care about right now:** the current era (The Life of a Showgirl), her CMA Awards performance on 18 November (her first in 13 years), the wedding aftermath, Chiefs games, awards season.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit, Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1), and Google. Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come first.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** age mix, app versus web, and which format earns shares (reach is 0 to 1 per post).
- **Traffic reality (week ending 2026-11-15):** 29 visitors, 45 pageviews; privacy 17 and support 4 are app review, home 21. Referrers: direct 37, Google 3, Bing 2, Facebook 2. The link counter read 45, but 29 carry an unknown campaign tag in a 2-post week, so only the traced families (16) count.

## How we grow (bets, ranked, each with the metric that proves/kills it)

This week all three news drafts (Lansing award, CMA performance, her two favourite albums) were stopped by the voice judge; the "news takes whichever day it lands" rule ([#6728](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6728)) failed at the gate, not in the rule.

   - Kills or reshapes it: three weeks of real news posts drawing fewer pageviews than evergreen posts — then news stays on the site. Not yet testable: no real news post has gone out.

   - Proves it: at least 5 pairs live a week and links from our posts reaching 40 pageviews a week by 2026-11-30 (14, 16, 20, then 16 traced).
   - Kills or reshapes it: under 20 a week on 2026-11-30 with daily posting and Facebook live — then Tree re-plans the formats. A zero-post week is a pipeline failure, not evidence against the formats.

## Content strategy

- **The craft is top tier:** the Showgirl Era Secrets ([#6780](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6780)) are what a fan shares — the crowd closing the album was recorded at the last Eras Tour show — each sourced; news runs read sources in full and mark single-source stories unconfirmed. Never trade that honesty for speed.
- **The gap is the current era's videos and pictures:** no Patient Zero video ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)), no Showgirl social photos ([#6644](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6644)), one Runway look ([#6339](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6339)); no desk works content issues ([#6517](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6517)). Favour stories over listings (tour dates are filler).
- **Write like a fan all the way through (new, 2026-11-16).** Every caption must clear the independent voice judge (every score 4+); its usual complaint is a warm first line over a news-recap middle. Check with the judge before opening a draft; rewrite on a rejection instead of going dark.
- **Photos first.** Real Taylor photography is the default; a shipped photo is rejected by a fingerprint check ([#6017](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6017)), so never-used photos are scarce: 50 new a week while the library is under 3,000 ([#5871](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5871)), Showgirl first. Every post carries a picture (text-only X banned 2026-10-30). Uncredited photos are fine; credit the photographer whenever known.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** An official video drop gets a videos entry and the embed on its moment the same day, dated to top the feed; on Instagram and X it ships as image-plus-text (guardrail 5).
- **News outranks the calendar:** at least 3 news moments a week on social ([#6311](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6311)), on whichever day each lands ([#6728](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6728)).
- **Never a dark day:** a week's standby subject backs every beat ([#6729](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6729)); the calendar ships in its own PR so it always lands.
- **Openers:** three repeated reaction-stems banned, first person kept ([#6743](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6743)). Instagram hashtags on by default, 8–12 mid-tail ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105)).
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month, weekly Blank Spaces timeline, monthly Mood beat, daily heartbeat, the news floor, the photo-viewer arc once its phone check runs ([#6699](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6699)). Each link's `utm_campaign` is the family ([#6524](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6524)).
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md`.

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
- **2026-10-19 — Experiment 01 (hashtags versus none on Instagram):** closed by Tree as unreadable — 2 of 5 pairs, both at reach 1; tags became the default and the comparison returns at a median reach of 10 ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105), kept by this review).
- **2026-10-30 — Text-only X posts:** banned by the owner; every post carries an image or video ([#6432](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6432)).
- **2026-11-01 — Waiting on a founder answer for reversible engineering:** the search-pages decision sat a week for an "A" the owner's 2026-10-09 amendment says Marjorie can give herself; she now decides reversible, no-spend calls and asks the owner only for money, secrets, logins or major strategy shifts.
- **2026-11-08 — Fixed news days on the calendar:** three set news days in the week of 11-02 produced 0 news posts, because the week's big moments landed on other days; news now takes whichever day it lands.
- **2026-11-08 — Re-running the auto-builder on tickets outside its files:** five re-dispatches of one ticket ([#5537](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5537)) produced nothing, because the ticket said in its own text that the builder may not edit those files; the fix is routing ([#6690](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6690)), not repetition.
