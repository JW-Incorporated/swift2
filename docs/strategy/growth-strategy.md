## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** fans have to be able to find us. Posting now works — 12 posts (6 Instagram/X pairs) went out this week with no failures — but they reached 7 accounts in total, because a 4-follower account posting into its own feed is seen by no one. The work moves from making posts to getting them seen: Facebook (our biggest outside referrer, and we post nothing there yet), same-day news on social, the awareness replies, and pages that Google can find.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-10-25):** not growing. 24 visitors against 41 the week before; most of the drop is the privacy page (25 pageviews to 6), which is not fan traffic. Links from our own posts brought 14 pageviews (2 the week before), Facebook 10, search 3. Followers flat (Instagram 4, X 2 to 1, Facebook 11).
- **Order of work:** (1) Facebook gets our posts (one 10-minute step by the owner), (2) the site's same-day news goes to social, (3) every story gets its own address Google can read (new bet, needs a spec), (4) the awareness replies get posted or paused, (5) Taylor's official videos lead (Patient Zero is still missing). Growth is priority #1.
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever once traffic is meaningful; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors, from 24 now to 100 by 2026-12-31 (lowered from 200). Leading indicators: Instagram followers 15 by 2026-11-15 and 50 by 2026-12-31; search referrals 25 pageviews a week by 2026-12-31.

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties of every age who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory-and-easter-egg readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see what happened in a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified (the "recent news" half of `docs/vision.md`); the time-travel by era (the other half); Taylor's own videos; fashion ("shop the look"); something worth sharing.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit (r/TaylorSwift and adjacent), Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1), and Google — fans search for the story everyone is talking about. Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come before any promo post.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** their age mix, how many are app-first versus web, and which format earns shares. Instagram reach is 0 to 2 accounts per post, so no format comparison is readable yet (Tree closed the hashtag experiment as unreadable for exactly this reason).
- **Traffic reality (week ending 2026-10-25):** 24 visitors, 30 pageviews. The home page took 22, the privacy page 6 (down from 25 — that was app-review or automated traffic, not fans). Referrers: direct 16, Facebook 10 (m.facebook.com 6, facebook.com 4), Google 2, DuckDuckGo 1. Links from our own posts: 14 pageviews (heartbeat 3, mood 2, thread 2, news and untagged families 7).

## How we grow (bets, ranked, each with the metric that proves/kills it)

1. **Daily paired posts on Instagram and X, plus the Facebook Page (Tree drafts and clears his own drafts via the signed `tree-auto` workflow — guardrail 1 as amended 2026-10-09).** The engine finally ran: 12 posts on 6 days, 0 failed, 4 of 4 approvals clean. The problem is now reach, not output — 7 Instagram accounts reached across 6 posts, 0 likes, saves or shares.
   - **Facebook is the cheapest reach we are not using.** Facebook sent 10 of our 30 pageviews and holds our biggest following (11), yet the Facebook Page got 0 posts because the Instagram token lacks the page-posting permission. The fix is one owner step (HUMAN-ACTIONS #138, about 10 minutes).
   - Proves it: at least 5 pairs live per week for two straight weeks (week 1 of 2 met: 6 pairs) and social-tagged pageviews rising in `social.byCampaign` (14 this week, 2 last week).
   - Kills or reshapes it: the 14-day clock started 2026-10-19 — by 2026-11-01, fewer than 5 social-tagged pageviews a week means the format is wrong, not the cadence. Week 1 read 14, so it stands.
2. **First and right on time-sensitive fan moments — and Taylor's own videos lead.** The site is fast: all 15 real-world moments this week were on the site within 48 hours, most within hours. Social carried 1 of 15 (the first live "The Fate of Ophelia", about 50 hours after it happened); the data script counts 3, but two of those were keyword matches against unrelated posts.
   - **Official Taylor videos lead the feed (owner direction 2026-10-11).** When an official video drops, the coverage IS the video: a videos entry (`supabase/seed/videos/`) plus the embed on the covering moment, the same day. The missing "visualizer" slot landed this week ([#6186](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6186)); the Patient Zero video is still not in the rail two weeks after the direction ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)).
   - Proves it: at least 3 of a week's time-sensitive moments on social within 48 hours (verified by reading the posts, not by keyword match); every official video drop in the videos rail within 48 hours.
   - Kills or reshapes it: news posts that draw less engagement than evergreen ones for three weeks running (unreadable so far — reach is 0 to 2 either way).
3. **Search finds us: every story gets its own address Google can read (new, 2026-10-25).** The whole site is one address (`/`); every era and moment loads behind it after the app starts, so search engines see a single page (`apps/web/app/sitemap.ts` says so). We ship several sourced stories a day that nobody can find by searching. Banked for a spec, then build ([#6306](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6306)).
   - Proves it: search referrals (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo pageviews in `traffic.topReferrers`) — 3 this week — reaching 25 a week by 2026-12-31, and rising every week after the pages ship.
   - Kills or reshapes it: 8 weeks after the pages ship with fewer than 10 search pageviews a week — then the pages are not what fans search for, and we change titles and topics before building more.
4. **The reach lane: awareness image replies first, then comments, follows, and Reddit/Facebook-group participation (the owner posts every reply; Tree finds the threads and writes every word).**
   - **Awareness image replies (owner direction 2026-10-01, built as the awareness lane in `docs/community/README.md`):** Tree delivers 10+ Reddit/Facebook threads a day to Discord, each with a picture of the site attached and a short reply. No link and no pitch: the goal is the reply "what is that?!", and then the owner explains.
   - Metric for this lane: opportunities delivered 32 this week (4.6 a day, up from 2.4, against the 10+ target), owner-posted 0 of 32 (0 of 88 across three measured weeks), "what is that?" replies unmeasured (none reported). The fix that filters out threads the owner cannot reply to and restores 10+ a day ([#6089](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6089)) is sized medium and waits for the owner's "Plan approved" comment, which is why the build desk keeps passing it.
   - Kills or reshapes this lane: two weeks with 10+ delivered a day and fewer than 3 "what is that?" replies means the picture or the thread type is wrong. The keep-or-pause clock set on 2026-10-18 ends 2026-11-01: if still 0 posted, the lane goes to the owner as a plain keep-or-pause choice — it is his direction, so only he stops it.
   - Proves it: Reddit non-promo contributions reach 20; Instagram followers reach 15 by 2026-11-15.
5. **Share cards: fans do the distribution.** "Share as image" on an era or moment and the personal "My Eras" card (`/api/share-card`, [#4668](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4668)); every `?item=` link already unfurls with that moment's picture.
   - Proves it: card-originated visits and logged shares rising week over week once the traffic exists (assumption: any measurable share in the first month is a win at this size). None logged to date; not re-measured this week.
   - Kills it: four weeks with zero logged shares once posts reach more than 10 accounts each — stop promoting cards and fold the effort into bet 2.
6. **App-store launch week (later; gated).** A realistic bump is +200 to +500 Instagram followers in 7 days, but only on a base of 150 or more (`docs/marketing/social-strategy.md` §3). Not started until bets 1 and 4 have built that base.
   - Proves it: a 7-day follower jump of that size. Kills it: launching on a sub-100 base — postpone instead.

Not a growth bet yet: **fashion monetisation.** There is nothing to monetise at 24 visitors a week. Merch groundwork continues (the official-store sync runs daily; affiliate joins wait on the owner, HUMAN-ACTIONS #126) and affiliate work stays held for counsel sign-off (HUMAN-ACTIONS #27).

## Content strategy

- **The craft is top tier:** this week's stories were sourced and honest about what could not be read — the debut's 20th-anniversary website tribute says plainly that taylorswift.com could not be read and cut a fan theory to the rumor box ([#6286](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6286)); the 14-year-old Taylor on Good Morning America gives no year because GMA's own write-up disagrees with itself ([#6305](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6305)). That honesty is the brand; never trade it for speed.
- **The gap is reach and video, not accuracy:** the site covered 15 of 15 moments, social 1. The owner's video rule is not yet met for Patient Zero.
- **Photos first — for images.** Real Taylor photography is the default image; a photo that has ever shipped is rejected by an image fingerprint check ([#6017](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6017)), so a deep library of never-used photos is the scarce resource. Tree's target is at least 50 new never-used photos a week while the library is under 3,000 ([#5871](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5871)); two concert-photo imports landed this week. Uncredited photos are fine; credit the photographer whenever known.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** The day Taylor releases an official video — music video, lyric video, visualizer (a slot for it exists since [#6186](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6186)), or official performance upload — the site leads with the video itself: a videos entry and the click-to-play embed on the covering moment (official player only) land the same day, dated so the drop tops the newest-first feed. On Instagram and X the drop still ships as an image-plus-text pair — guardrail 5 permits no video posting — written to send fans to the video on the site.
- **Hashtags are on by default on Instagram** (8–12 mid-tail tags; never mega-tags) — Tree closed Experiment 01 as unreadable at reach 1 and made tags the default ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105)); the comparison returns when median Instagram reach reaches 10.
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month (one paired beat per thread window, plus at most one make-good a month for a hero the pipeline ate — [#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105)), the weekly Blank Spaces timeline (confirmed-only), the monthly Mood beat, the daily heartbeat, and the same-day news beat. One idea goes to Instagram and X together, and a beat is drafted by its own day's run — never parked more than 24h out.
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md` (a signed approval stamp before anything posts — Tree's own `tree-auto` stamp since 2026-10-09, the owner's ✅ as fallback — rights, no AI images of Taylor, confirmed-only sensitive topics, platform limits including no video posts on IG/X, replies and DMs human for now).

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
- **2026-10-19 — Experiment 01 (hashtags versus none on Instagram):** closed by Tree as unreadable — 2 of 5 pairs, both at reach 1; tags became the default and the comparison returns at a median reach of 10 ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105), kept by this review).
