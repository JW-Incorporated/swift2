## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** social drives to the site. We post one genuinely shareable thing every day where fans already are (Instagram and X, paired), take part in fan communities as real members, and give fans a card they want to post themselves; the site is where they land.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-10-11):** still not growing, but the machine finally moved. 42 visitors this week (29 the week before — mostly non-fan traffic: the privacy page took 27 of 69 pageviews and nearly every visit is "direct"). Two posts shipped — the first since the rebuild — with a third pair clearing at the week's edge, and for the first time we can see social sending site visits (12 tagged pageviews). But the engine broke twice more: Tree's self-clear lane lacked its dispatch token for two days (fixed, two drafts stranded), and the routine that writes Tree's weekly calendar has died twice running — the calendar ran out 2026-10-11.
- **Order of work:** (1) Tree's weekly plan and calendar ship — they are the input the whole posting engine drafts from, (2) a full seven-day posting week with zero stranded drafts, (3) the owner posts the first awareness replies (39 are waiting), (4) same-day coverage of fan moments with official Taylor videos leading the feed, (5) share cards as a loop. Growth is priority #1.
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever once traffic is meaningful; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors, from 42 now to 500 by 2026-12-31 (assumption — confirmed or replaced each week). Leading indicator: Instagram followers 150 by 2026-11-30 (moved from 2026-10-31 — we sit at 4 with the engine live only days of October), 500 by 2026-12-31 (kept, flagged at risk until two straight full posting weeks exist).

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties of every age who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory-and-easter-egg readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see what happened in a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified (the "recent news" half of `docs/vision.md`); the time-travel by era (the other half); fashion ("shop the look"); something worth sharing.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit (r/TaylorSwift and adjacent), Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1). Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come before any promo post.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** their age mix, how many are app-first versus web, and which format earns shares. Instagram reach/saves/shares per post arrive automatically, and as of this week per-campaign site visits are finally measured first-party (utm-tagged page loads — 12 this week). What a "good" number looks like at our size is still unknown.
- **Traffic reality:** 42 visitors this week, nearly all "direct", and the privacy page took 27 of 69 pageviews — which looks like automated or app-review traffic, not fans. The 12 social-tagged pageviews are the first evidence any channel sends anyone; no channel sends fans at volume yet.

## How we grow (bets, ranked, each with the metric that proves/kills it)

1. **Daily paired posts on Instagram and X (Tree drafts and clears his own drafts via the signed `tree-auto` workflow — guardrail 1 as amended 2026-10-09; the owner's ✅ remains only as a fallback).** Distribution is the engine; it produced 2 posts this week against a plan of ~7.
   - Proves it: at least 5 posts live per week for two straight weeks, and social-tagged visits appear in `social.byCampaign` (now measurable — 12 pageviews this week).
   - Kills or reshapes it: 14 days of daily posting with fewer than 5 social-sourced visits a week means the format is wrong, not the cadence — change formats before posting more.
   - Now: the self-clear lane works end to end as of 2026-10-11 — the missing dispatch token that stranded the 10-09 and 10-10 drafts ([#5443](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5443), [#5495](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5495)) was fixed ([#5521](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5521)) and the 10-11 pair cleared and posted the same day. The live blocker is upstream: the routine that writes Tree's weekly plan and 14-day calendar has died on its turn ceiling twice ([#5470](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5470), escalated with a ready-to-paste fix prompt), and the calendar ran out 2026-10-11 — no calendar, nothing to draft.
2. **The reach lane: awareness image replies first, then comments, follows, and Reddit/Facebook-group participation (the owner posts every reply; Tree finds the threads and writes every word).** Near-zero traction is a symptom of this engine never running.
   - **Awareness image replies (owner direction 2026-10-01, built as the awareness lane in `docs/community/README.md`):** Tree delivers 10+ Reddit/Facebook threads a day to Discord, each with a picture of the site (a share card for the moment or era under discussion) attached and a short reply written as if the reader had seen the site before. No link and no pitch: the goal is the reply "what is that?!", and then the owner explains.
   - Metric for this lane, first measured week: opportunities delivered 39 this week (5.6/day against the 10+ target — delivery works, volume is short), owner-posted 0 of 39, and "what is that?" replies unreported (the owner reports these in `#longlive-marjorie`; none were reported, so this is unmeasured). The lane's bottleneck is now posting, not delivery — the third week running this has waited on the owner's hands.
   - Kills or reshapes this lane: two weeks with 10+ delivered a day and fewer than 3 "what is that?" replies means the picture or the thread type is wrong; change which card and which thread types, not the volume.
   - Proves it: Reddit non-promo contributions reach 20; Instagram followers reach 150 by 2026-11-30.
   - Kills or reshapes it: three weeks of the lane running with no follower or referrer movement means the communities or the angle are wrong — switch communities, do not add effort.
3. **Share cards: fans do the distribution.** "Share as image" on an era or moment and the personal "My Eras" card (W9, `/api/share-card`, [#4668](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4668)); Tree may also post them as a sanctioned image kind.
   - Proves it: card-originated visits and logged shares rising week over week once the traffic exists (assumption: any measurable share in the first month is a win at this size). Zero logged so far; counting continues.
   - Kills it: four weeks with zero logged shares — stop promoting cards and fold the effort into bet 4.
4. **First and right on time-sensitive fan moments.** Fans come back daily to the place that covered the thing everyone is arguing about, accurately and within hours.
   - **Official Taylor videos lead the feed (owner direction 2026-10-11).** When an official video drops, the coverage IS the video: a videos entry (`supabase/seed/videos/`, synced to the reader by `scripts/sync-longlive-videos.mjs`) plus the embed on the covering moment land the same day, so the drop tops the newest-first era feed and sits under the Videos chip. Covering a video drop with photos alone is a miss against the 48-hour metric below, not a judgment call.
   - Proves it: time-sensitive events covered on the site within 48 hours — this week 12 of 21 events have a verified merged PR (six of them within 6 hours of intake, the best yet; 9 more closed without a verifiable ship) — and on social within hours (1 of 21 this week). For video drops specifically: every official drop live on the site with the video embedded within 48 hours. The standing "Patient Zero" test currently fails on exactly this: the official Patient Zero video is still missing from the Showgirl videos rail ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)).
   - Kills or reshapes it: news posts that draw less approval and engagement than evergreen ones for three weeks running.
5. **App-store launch week (later; gated).** A realistic bump is +200 to +500 Instagram followers in 7 days, but only on a base of 150 or more (`docs/marketing/social-strategy.md` §3). Not started until bet 1 and 2 have built that base.
   - Proves it: a 7-day follower jump of that size. Kills it: launching on a sub-100 base — postpone instead.

Not a growth bet yet: **fashion monetisation.** There is nothing to monetise at 42 visitors a week; "shop the look" and the dead-link cleanup ([#4643](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4643), merged) protect trust until traffic exists. Affiliate work stays held for counsel sign-off (HUMAN-ACTIONS #27).

## Content strategy

- **The craft is already top tier:** sourced to several outlets, honest about what could not be verified, balanced, and rumours are re-dated rather than quietly deleted. This week's track dossiers carry verbatim quotes with a source per claim and label critics' opinions as opinions; the vault runs harden sources and re-verify theories on schedule. That honesty is the brand; never trade it for speed.
- **The gap is shareability, not accuracy:** most of this week's 20 content PRs were maintenance fans never see (privacy redlines, source hardening, date refreshes). The dossiers are genuinely deep; nothing on social points fans at them yet. Content built to be shared — and the video rail staying current the day a drop lands — is what is missing.
- **Photos first — for images.** Real Taylor photography is the default image ("the Taylor-photo standard"); a photo that has ever shipped is rejected, so a deep library of never-used photos is the scarce resource. Growing it, credited or not, is a standing owner priority (credit the photographer whenever known; unknown credits no longer block a post — carried into the gates by [#4706](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4706)). Cards and site screens are secondary image kinds Tree may use by evidence. Photos-first is the default for the *image*, never a cap on video coverage — see the next bullet.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** The day Taylor releases an official video — a music video, lyric video, or official performance upload — the site leads with the video itself: a videos entry and the click-to-play embed on the covering moment (official first-party player only, per the reader's standing rule) land the same day, dated so the drop tops the newest-first feed. Photo-only enrichment of a video drop is the failure mode this direction retires. On Instagram and X the drop still ships as an image-plus-text pair — guardrail 5 permits no video posting — written to send fans to the video on the site.
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month (all six threads, now one paired beat per thread window — Tree's 2026-10-10 correction), the weekly Blank Spaces timeline (confirmed-only), the monthly Mood beat, the daily heartbeat, and the same-day news beat. One idea goes to Instagram and X together, and a beat is drafted by its own day's run — never parked more than 24h out (the parking rule killed the only Mood pair ever drafted).
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md` (a signed approval stamp before anything posts — Tree's own `tree-auto` stamp since 2026-10-09, the owner's ✅ as fallback — rights, no AI images of Taylor, confirmed-only sensitive topics, platform limits including no video posts on IG/X, replies and DMs human for now).

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
