## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** fans have to be able to find us. Posting came back this week (12 posts, a post on six of seven days, no failures) and growth did not move at all — so the next step is being found (search pages, the Facebook Page), not posting more.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-11-22):** not growing. 27 visitors against 28; the home page had 18, the privacy page 12 (app review, not fans). Every Instagram post we could measure reached 0 accounts. Followers flat an eighth week (Instagram 4, X 1, Facebook 11). Search sent 1 visit (5 the week before).
- **Why we are stuck:** the site is one address Google cannot break into stories, and our accounts have almost no followers, so what we post reaches nobody. Search pages wait on one pasted prompt (HUMAN-ACTIONS #152, four weeks); the Facebook Page waits on a 10-minute setting (HUMAN-ACTIONS #138, five weeks).
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors — checkpoint 40 on 2026-11-30 (expected miss), 100 moved to 2027-01-31 and tied to the search pages going live by 2026-12-14. Leading indicators: search 25 pageviews a week by 2027-01-31 (1 now); links from our posts 40 pageviews a week by 2026-11-30 (14 traced now); Instagram followers 10 by 2026-12-31 (a weak proxy).

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified; the time-travel by era (`docs/vision.md`); Taylor's own videos; fashion; something worth sharing.
- **What they care about right now:** the current era (The Life of a Showgirl), her CMA Awards performance of "I Knew It, I Knew You" into "Our Song" (18 November), the 69th Grammy nominations, the Toy Story 5 song's awards run, the wedding aftermath, whether the debut re-record is coming.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit, Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1), and Google. Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come first.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** age mix, app versus web, and which format earns shares (reach is 0 per post this week).
- **Traffic reality (week ending 2026-11-22):** 27 visitors, 39 pageviews; privacy 17 pageviews and support 6 are app review, home 18. Referrers: direct 32 pageviews, Facebook 7 (with no posts from us there), Bing 1, Google 0. Links from our posts: 16 pageviews, 14 traceable to a format (daily posts 8, threads 5, timeline 1).

## Content strategy

- **The craft is top tier:** this week's runs resolved open rumours against what actually happened (the Grammy Best Country Song forecast marked as missed, the 2027 festival headliner question settled), and the CMA performance landed with the official video and full sourcing. That honesty is the product. Never trade it for speed.
- **Favour stories over filler:** a fan's first CMA performance in 13 years, an insect named after Taylor, Travis on her first Chiefs game — yes. Luxury-tax hearings in two states and an Ohio landscaping board — accurate but nothing a fan shares; keep them short.
- **The gap is still the current era's videos and pictures:** no Patient Zero video ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)), no Showgirl photos for social ([#7073](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/7073), [#6644](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6644)), one Runway look ([#6339](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6339)); content issues still have no desk ([#6517](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6517), re-dispatched 2026-11-22).
- **Write like a fan all the way through:** the feeling opens, the detail follows (Tree's Rule 5, [#6911](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6911)). Every caption must clear the independent voice judge (every score 4+); a rejection gets one rewrite ([#6904](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6904)). Current-era facts must get past the judge when our sources back them ([#7080](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/7080)).
- **Photos first.** Real Taylor photography is the default; a shipped photo is rejected by a fingerprint check ([#6017](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6017)), so never-used photos are scarce: 50 new a week while the library is under 3,000 ([#5871](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5871)), Showgirl first, and a news post uses a photo of that moment when one exists. Every post carries a picture (text-only X banned 2026-10-30). Uncredited photos are fine; credit the photographer whenever known.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** An official video drop gets a videos entry and the embed on its moment the same day, dated to top the feed; on Instagram and X it ships as image-plus-text (guardrail 5).
- **News outranks the calendar — every evergreen beat, the timeline included:** at least 3 news moments a week on social ([#6311](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6311)), on whichever day each lands ([#6728](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6728)), in the next free slot or the next evergreen slot, whichever comes first.
- **Never a dark day:** a week's standby subject backs every beat ([#6729](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6729)); the calendar ships in its own two-file PR so it always lands ([#6899](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6899)).
- **Openers:** three repeated reaction-stems banned, first person kept ([#6743](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6743)). Instagram hashtags on by default, 8–12 mid-tail ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105)).
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month, weekly Blank Spaces timeline, monthly Mood beat, daily heartbeat, the news floor, the photo-viewer arc once its phone check runs ([#6699](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6699)). Each link's `utm_campaign` is the family ([#6524](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6524)).
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md`.

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
- **2026-10-19 — Experiment 01 (hashtags versus none on Instagram):** closed by Tree as unreadable — 2 of 5 pairs, both at reach 1; tags became the default and the comparison returns at a median reach of 10 ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105), kept by this review).
- **2026-10-30 — Text-only X posts:** banned by the owner; every post carries an image or video ([#6432](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6432)).
- **2026-11-01 — Waiting on a founder answer for reversible engineering:** the search-pages decision sat a week for an "A" the owner's 2026-10-09 amendment says Marjorie can give herself; she now decides reversible, no-spend calls and asks the owner only for money, secrets, logins or major strategy shifts.
- **2026-11-08 — Fixed news days on the calendar:** three set news days in the week of 11-02 produced 0 news posts, because the week's big moments landed on other days; news now takes whichever day it lands.
- **2026-11-08 — Re-running the auto-builder on tickets outside its files:** five re-dispatches of one ticket ([#5537](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5537)) produced nothing, because the ticket said in its own text that the builder may not edit those files; the fix is routing ([#6690](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6690)), not repetition.
