## Summary

- **Who we serve:** devoted Taylor Swift fans who want news they can trust (sourced, fake stories flagged) and a way to time-travel through her eras — people who argue about details and share what is true.
- **The core bet:** fans have to be able to find us. We make good things and almost nobody sees them: 12 posts this week reached 3 Instagram accounts in total, and the site's stories cannot be found on Google. Every lever that would change that — Facebook posting, pages Google can read, news on social — sat still this week. This week is about unsticking them, mostly without waiting on the owner.
- **Where we are (week ending 2026-11-01):** not growing. 22 visitors against 24; 42 pageviews against 30, but over half were the privacy and support pages (34), which are app-review traffic, not fans — the home page had 13 visitors. Facebook sent 6 pageviews (10 the week before), search 2 (3). Links from our own posts brought 16. Followers flat for the fifth week (Instagram 4, X 1 to 2, Facebook 11).
- **Order of work:** (1) news the site already covers goes to social — the dispatcher started 0 drafts this week, (2) the Facebook Page gets our posts (one 10-minute step by the owner), (3) every story gets its own address Google can read (decided this week; one pasted prompt starts it), (4) content gaps fans would notice — starting with the Patient Zero video — get a desk that works them. Growth is priority #1.
- **Money:** later. The fashion section is the long-term revenue lever once traffic is meaningful; we revisit it at a sustained ~2,000 visitors a week (assumption) and only after the owner's counsel sign-off.
- **This quarter's target metric:** weekly unique visitors, from 22 now to 100 by 2026-12-31. Leading indicators: social-tagged pageviews 40 a week by 2026-11-30 (16 now); search pageviews 25 a week by 2026-12-31 (2 now); Instagram followers 10 by 2026-11-30 and 25 by 2026-12-31 (a weak proxy, lowered — see Changelog).

## Audience

- **Who:** Swifties of every age who follow Taylor closely enough to care whether a rumour is true: era loyalists, theory-and-easter-egg readers, fashion followers, and fans who want to see what happened in a given month of an era.
- **What they come for:** the latest news, verified (the "recent news" half of `docs/vision.md`); the time-travel by era (the other half); Taylor's own videos; fashion ("shop the look"); something worth sharing.
- **Where they are:** Instagram, X, TikTok, Reddit (r/TaylorSwift and adjacent), Tumblr, Facebook groups (`docs/marketing/growth-plan.md` §1), and Google — fans search for the story everyone is talking about. Reddit and Tumblr punish promotion: 20 to 30 genuine zero-link contributions come before any promo post.
- **What we do not know (assumption):** their age mix, how many are app-first versus web, and which format earns shares. Instagram reach was 0 to 1 accounts per post this week, so no format comparison is readable.
- **Traffic reality (week ending 2026-11-01):** 22 visitors, 42 pageviews. Privacy page 22 pageviews (12 visitors) and support page 12 (8) — app-review or automated traffic, not fans; home page 14 (13 visitors). Referrers: direct 40, Facebook 6 (facebook.com 4, m.facebook.com 2), DuckDuckGo 2, Google 0. Links from our own posts: 16 pageviews, 13 of them under a campaign name outside Tree's five families, so which format sent them is unreadable.

## How we grow (bets, ranked, each with the metric that proves/kills it)

1. **Daily paired posts on Instagram and X, plus the Facebook Page (Tree drafts and clears his own drafts via the signed `tree-auto` workflow — guardrail 1 as amended 2026-10-09).** Output held: 12 posts on 6 days, 0 failed, even though the Claude account's usage limit took the drafting run dark on 10-27 and 10-28. Reach did not: 3 Instagram accounts in total, 0 likes, saves or shares.
   - **Facebook is still the cheapest reach we are not using.** Facebook sent 6 of our 42 pageviews and holds our biggest following (11), and the Page got 0 posts again because the Instagram token lacks the page-posting permission (HUMAN-ACTIONS #138, about 10 minutes of the owner's time, open two weeks).
   - Proves it: at least 5 pairs live per week (met two weeks running: 6 and 6) and social-tagged pageviews rising in `social.byCampaign` (14, then 16), toward 40 a week by 2026-11-30.
   - Kills or reshapes it: the 14-day clock ended 2026-11-01 with 16 social-tagged pageviews against a kill line of 5, so the bet stands. New line: if social-tagged pageviews are still under 20 a week on 2026-11-30 with Facebook posting live, the formats are wrong and Tree re-plans them from what sent the clicks.
2. **First and right on time-sensitive fan moments — and Taylor's own videos lead.** The site is fast: 18 of 27 real-world moments this week were on the site within 48 hours (22 eventually), many within an hour. Social carried none: the data script counts 3, but all three were keyword matches against evergreen posts (a ring challenge, a runway quiz, a deep cut). The cause is now known: the news dispatcher ran every few hours and started 0 drafts, skipping stories as "already covered" by unrelated posts (#6485, #6484, #6448) and never promoting the 6 to 7 site-fronted moments a day.
   - **Official Taylor videos lead the feed (owner direction 2026-10-11).** When an official video drops, the coverage IS the video: a videos entry (`supabase/seed/videos/`) plus the embed on the covering moment, the same day. Patient Zero is still not in the rail three weeks after the direction ([#5508](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/5508)), because no routine reads content-desk issues ([#6517](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6517)). Newer moments do carry official videos (the Toy Story 5 screening story embeds "I Knew It, I Knew You").
   - Proves it: at least 3 of a week's time-sensitive moments on social within 48 hours (verified by reading the posts, not by keyword match); every official video drop in the videos rail within 48 hours.
   - Kills or reshapes it: news posts that draw less engagement than evergreen ones for three weeks running — not yet testable, because no news post has gone out for two weeks.
3. **Search finds us: every story gets its own address Google can read.** The whole site is one address (`/`), so search engines see a single page. Search sent 2 pageviews this week. Decided "A — spec now, build next" by Marjorie on 2026-11-01 under the owner's 2026-10-09 amendment (reversible, no spend) after a week waiting for a founder answer; no bot can write the spec, so it starts from a prompt the owner pastes into one Claude Code session ([#6306](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6306)).
   - Proves it: search referrals (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo pageviews in `traffic.topReferrers`) — 2 this week — reaching 25 a week by 2026-12-31, and rising every week after the pages ship.
   - Kills or reshapes it: 8 weeks after the pages ship with fewer than 10 search pageviews a week — then the pages are not what fans search for, and we change titles and topics before building more.
4. **The reach lane: awareness image replies, then comments, follows, and Reddit/Facebook-group participation (the owner posts every reply; Tree finds the threads and writes every word).**
   - **Awareness image replies (owner direction 2026-10-01, built as the awareness lane in `docs/community/README.md`):** Tree delivers Reddit/Facebook threads to Discord, each with a picture of the site attached and a short reply. No link and no pitch: the goal is the reply "what is that?!", and then the owner explains.
   - Metric for this lane: opportunities delivered 21 this week (3.0 a day, down from 4.6, against the 10+ target), owner-posted 0 of 21 (0 of about 109 across four measured weeks), "what is that?" replies unmeasured (none reported).
   - **The keep-or-pause date (2026-11-01) has arrived with 0 posted.** It is the owner's direction, so only he stops it: the choice is in this week's plan, with Marjorie's recommendation to pause delivery until he has time to post, and to spend the effort on bets 2 and 3.    - Kills or reshapes this lane: the owner pauses it, or two weeks with 10+ delivered a day and fewer than 3 "what is that?" replies.
   - Proves it: Reddit non-promo contributions reach 20; "what is that?" replies reported in `#longlive-marjorie`.
5. **Share cards: fans do the distribution.** "Share as image" on an era or moment and the personal "My Eras" card (`/api/share-card`, [#4668](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/4668)); every `?item=` link already unfurls with that moment's picture.
   - Proves it: card-originated visits and logged shares rising week over week once the traffic exists (assumption: any measurable share in the first month is a win at this size). None logged to date; not re-measured this week.

6. **App-store launch week (later; gated).** A realistic bump is +200 to +500 Instagram followers in 7 days, but only on a base of 150 or more (`docs/marketing/social-strategy.md` §3). Not started until bets 1 and 4 have built that base.
   - Proves it: a 7-day follower jump of that size. Kills it: launching on a sub-100 base — postpone instead.

Not a growth bet yet: **fashion monetisation.** There is nothing to monetise at 22 visitors a week. Merch groundwork continues (the official-store sync runs daily; affiliate joins wait on the owner, HUMAN-ACTIONS #126) and affiliate work stays held for counsel sign-off (HUMAN-ACTIONS #27).

## Content strategy

- **The craft is top tier:** the Toy Story 5 screening story ([#6513](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6513)) was on the site within an hour, quotes Taylor from a source that loaded, drops the outlet it could not read, cuts details about a minor, embeds the official song, and links back to the "Fifteen" moment about the same friend — the time-travel half and the news half in one page. Nelly's $50,000 McDonald's-bag story ([#6489](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6489)) was filed under the 2011 Houston show it happened at, not as loose gossip. That honesty and placement are the brand; never trade them for speed.
- **The gap is reach and the backlog of known holes, not accuracy:** social carried 0 of 27 moments; the Patient Zero video, the Debut track guide (7 of 14 songs empty on its 20th anniversary), the Love Story thread and the Runway's recent eras are all filed and none has a desk that works them ([#6517](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/issues/6517)).
- **Photos first — for images.** Real Taylor photography is the default image; a photo that has ever shipped is rejected by an image fingerprint check ([#6017](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6017)), so a deep library of never-used photos is the scarce resource. Tree's target is at least 50 new never-used photos a week while the library is under 3,000 ([#5871](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/5871)); two concert-photo imports landed this week, and every post now carries a picture (the owner banned text-only X posts on 2026-10-30, [#6432](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6432)). Uncredited photos are fine; credit the photographer whenever known.
- **Videos lead when the video is the story (owner direction 2026-10-11).** The day Taylor releases an official video — music video, lyric video, visualizer (a slot exists since [#6186](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6186)), or official performance upload — the site leads with the video itself: a videos entry and the click-to-play embed on the covering moment (official player only) land the same day, dated so the drop tops the newest-first feed. On Instagram and X the drop still ships as an image-plus-text pair — guardrail 5 permits no video posting — written to send fans to the video on the site.
- **News is a weekly floor, not a ceiling (Tree, [#6311](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6311)):** at least 3 news moments a week on social; a news reserve always drafts its fallback ([#6321](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6321)); the dispatcher looks back 48 hours and promotes moments the site already fronts ([#6314](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6314)). The rules are right; the dispatcher's "already covered" match is what is failing them.
- **Hashtags are on by default on Instagram** (8–12 mid-tail tags; never mega-tags) — the comparison returns when median Instagram reach reaches 10 ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105)).
- **Campaign rhythm (Tree owns the calendar):** thread of the month (one paired beat per thread window, plus at most one make-good a month for a hero the pipeline ate), the weekly Blank Spaces timeline (confirmed-only), the monthly Mood beat, the daily heartbeat, and the news floor. One idea goes to Instagram and X together, and a beat is drafted by its own day's run — never parked more than 24h out. Every post link carries its campaign family in `utm_campaign`, so the site can count which format sent the visit.
- **Hard limits that never move without the owner:** `docs/social/guardrails.md` (a signed approval stamp before anything posts — Tree's own `tree-auto` stamp since 2026-10-09, the owner's ✅ as fallback — rights, no AI images of Taylor, confirmed-only sensitive topics, platform limits including no video posts on IG/X, replies and DMs human for now).

## What we stopped and why

- **2026-08-11 — Generic era-art tiles as the Instagram image:** a 2026-08-11 audit found every image was one; real photos are the standard now.
- **2026-08-11 — "Did you know…" openers:** 12 of 14 posts opened that way; the opener is banned and openers are checked in code.
- **2026-08-11 — Planning Reels/Stories slots:** the pipeline posts a single image plus text, so those slots could never ship; none are planned.
- **2026-07-17 — Embedded Instagram/Facebook feeds on the site:** heavy third-party code that looks stale the moment posting slows; footer links only.
- **2026-10-01 — The owner writing social taste rules:** he reviews and approves; Tree and Marjorie decide, Fable rules disputes.
- **2026-10-01 — Paid X API metrics:** declined (spend needs the owner).
- **2026-10-01 — Chasing real names for uncredited photos:** the owner ruled uncredited photos fine; the effort goes into more photos, not better credits.
- **2026-10-11 — Photo-only enrichment of official video drops:** the owner's 2026-10-11 direction; when the video is the story, the video leads the feed and photos support it, not the reverse.
- **2026-10-11 — Parking an approved beat more than 24h ahead:** the staleness clock runs from approval, so an early-cleared post for a later slot dies unposted (it killed the 2026-10-03 Mood pair); a beat is drafted and cleared by its own day's run (Tree's rule, kept by this review).
- **2026-10-19 — Experiment 01 (hashtags versus none on Instagram):** closed by Tree as unreadable — 2 of 5 pairs, both at reach 1; tags became the default and the comparison returns at a median reach of 10 ([#6105](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6105), kept by this review).
- **2026-10-30 — Text-only X posts:** banned by the owner; every post carries an image or video ([#6432](https://github.com/JW-Incorporated/swift2/pull/6432)).
- **2026-11-01 — Waiting on a founder answer for reversible engineering:** the search-pages decision sat a week for an "A" the owner's 2026-10-09 amendment says Marjorie can give herself; she now decides reversible, no-spend calls and asks the owner only for money, secrets, logins or major strategy shifts.
