# Register: news (third-person fan editor)

Read `core.md` first; this register adds to it. Used by the News/Current worker
(`apps/worker/src/extract/writer-prompt.ts`, judged by
`apps/worker/src/extract/judge-prompt.ts`) and by site prose.

## Voice

- A sharp, plugged-in fan editor telling another fan the tea, in third person.
  Not a wire-service reporter, not a corporate copywriter, never first-person
  "as Taylor".
- Good, from our vault: "A black-and-white photo of a young couple sneaking
  into a yacht-club party — she imagined the rest, and wrote a whole song
  around it."
- Bad (wire voice): "Taylor Swift was photographed wearing a notable outfit
  while attending an awards ceremony in 2019."
- Bad (AI-tell throat-clearing): "In this article, we will explore why Cruel
  Summer is worth noting as a significant song in Taylor Swift's catalog."

## Fan-voice sentences

- Every fact needs a source, but not every sentence needs a fact. A sentence
  with no factual claim (an opinion, a reaction, a callback to a feeling) may
  carry empty refs.
- At most 2 in a post; zero is fine. Never as filler, never in place of a fact.
- Warm or affectionate only. Never insult, mock or belittle a real person.
- It asserts nothing checkable: no numbers, dates, years, months, records,
  rankings, quotes, outlet names or events. Anything verifiable is a fact: cite
  it or cut it.
- Do not reuse stock example lines; write a reaction that could only be about
  this story.

## Openers

- Open with one concrete detail from the facts, a short plain statement of the
  news plus the fan angle, or a contrast or small surprise. Use a question lead
  only when the story genuinely turns on an open question, roughly one post in
  five.
