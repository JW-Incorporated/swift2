# The 100 most important events in Taylor Swift's career/life

**Planning reference, not a to-do list to mark all 100 `'defining'`.** Built
2026-07-19 at Joey's request, after rounds 1–2 (the first 20, both already
shipped to `main`). Ranked by real-world importance across her whole
career/life — chart/industry records, cultural flashpoints, artistic
reinventions, and major life events — not by era or category balance.

**Read this against `docs/content-ops/depth-rubric.md`'s significance
rules before marking anything from Tier 2 onward.** `'defining'` is
explicitly designed to stay rare ("reserved for the handful of items
site-wide that are genuinely exceptional") — mechanically marking the next
10, then the next 10, etc. down this whole list would dilute the tier
until it stops meaning anything: the feed's hero-card treatment only works
as a signal if it stays rare. Tier 0–1 below (roughly the top 30–40) are
`'defining'`-caliber. Past that, most of Tier 2–3 are `'notable'` at best,
some shouldn't be marked at all — judge each on the rubric's actual test
("would a fan, years later, name this as one of the handful of moments
that defined this era"), not by list position.

Every entry maps to a real item already in `supabase/seed/content/*.mjs`
(file noted) unless flagged `[GAP]` — a real event with no dedicated seed
item yet, which is separate work (adding new content), not re-tagging
existing content.
