## 0. The mental model (read this first)

**Social drives to the site — not the other way around.** Nobody types a URL
they've never heard of. Discovery happens where Swifties already scroll
(TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, Tumblr); the site is the *destination* those
channels point at. Practical consequences:

- The site needs only lightweight links **to** our socials (footer icons — a
  trust signal and a follow loop). It does **not** need embedded Instagram
  or Facebook feeds. Embeds add heavy third-party code and trackers, slow
  the page, look stale the moment posting slows, and fight our static-site
  cost discipline. Verdict: **footer icon links yes, embedded feeds no.**
- Every social profile links to **https://www.longlivets.com/** with UTM
  tags (§8) so we can see which channel actually sends people.
- "Going viral" is not a launch-day event. The realistic shape (this is the
  well-documented recipe): a *seeding period* where we participate in fan
  communities as genuine members, a steady Reels/TikTok cadence where each
  video is a lottery ticket, and community word-of-mouth when the product
  genuinely delights. Virality compounds from consistency; it is not
  summoned on day one.

**What makes us lucky:** the Vault already contains hundreds of sourced,
researched moments, theories, easter-egg case files, and track dossiers.
Most new accounts starve for content ideas; ours are pre-written. The whole
content engine below is "excerpt the product."

## 4. The content engine — how posts get made, end to end

> **Superseded 2026-08-11 by [`social-strategy.md`](social-strategy.md).** Kept
> for history: §4's pillar *names* leaked into caption copy ("did you know")
> and produced 12 near-identical posts in 14 days. The pillars survive in the
> strategy file's daily heartbeat, with hook rules attached.

The pipeline (#518's rails, made concrete):

1. **Draft.** The Growth desk turns Vault content into platform-native
   drafts (caption + asset spec + suggested post time + one-line "why").
   Batches of 5-10 at a time, written into the social queue.
2. **Approve.** Drafts surface in the Founders' Brief social-queue section
   (urgent ones also as `founder-decision` issues). You reply
   approve / edit / kill — approving takes seconds because each draft
   carries its "why".
3. **Post.** You (or Wyatt) post approved drafts — manually at first
   (~10 min/day). Once that's annoying, grant **per-channel** scheduling
   through Meta Business Suite (that grant = its own decision entry, per
   the charter) and the queue becomes "approve → it schedules itself."
4. **Replies stay human forever.** The desk drafts suggested replies in the
   brief when a comment deserves one; a human sends or ignores.

**Content pillars** (all excerpted from the product, which is the moat):

| Pillar | Source in the Vault | Format |
|--------|---------------------|--------|
| **On this day in swiftory** | any moment whose date matches today | IG carousel / X post; the daily heartbeat |
| **Era deep cuts** | Era Secrets, month items | carousel (image + 2-3 slide story) |
| **Theory / clowning case files** | Mastermind case files, theories | Reels/TikTok talking-format; *the viral lane* |
| **Did-you-know track facts** | track dossiers | short Reels, X threads |
| **Symbol threads** | Invisible Strings motif atlas | carousel series ("every time she planted 13…") |
| **Product peeks** | screen-recordings of the actual site | Reels ("watch what happens when you scrub from Debut to TTPD") |

**Repurposing rule (industry-standard, verbatim):** one piece of content →
three to five formats. A theory case file becomes a TikTok script, an IG
carousel, an X thread, and a Tumblr post. Never write for one platform only,
and never paste identical copy across platforms — each gets native phrasing.

## 6. Instagram — the full worked example (#518's primary ask)

> **Cadence superseded 2026-08-11** — see [`social-strategy.md`](social-strategy.md)
> §2 for the real slot grammar (1 IG + 2 X per day). The "3-5 Reels/week" line
> below cannot be executed by any agent: the pipeline posts one image plus text.
> The growth *mechanics* here (engage outward, shares as the target metric,
> keyword-rich captions, collabs, evening-US timing) are still right and still
> apply.

**Setup before following anyone:** 9-post grid (3 era deep-cut carousels,
3 on-this-day posts, 2 theory case-file carousels, 1 site-peek Reel),
bio kit from §3, story highlights for "What is this?" and "Eras". An empty
account that follows people looks like a bot; a full one looks like a find.

**Cadence (steady state):** 3-5 Reels/week (the discovery engine — Reels
earn roughly double the reach of static posts) + 2-3 carousels/week (the
depth engine — carousels get saved, and saves compound) + daily Stories
(polls: "which era are you in today?", this-or-that, countdowns — Stories
nurture the followers we already have).

**Growth mechanics — how follows actually happen from zero:**

1. **Engage outward 30-60 min/day** (human or human-approved, never
   automated): leave genuinely funny/insightful comments *as the account*
   on Taylor's posts, big fan accounts, and — most productively — mid-size
   fan accounts (1k-50k), whose audiences actually read comments. A witty
   comment on a fresh big-account post is the single cheapest discovery
   mechanism a zero-follower account has.
2. **Follow ~25-50 relevant accounts/day, max** — fan accounts, era
   aesthetic accounts, tour photographers. Some follow back; more
   importantly the algorithm learns our neighborhood. **Never**
   follow/unfollow churn, never buy followers (both poison reach
   permanently and violate the charter).
3. **Shares are the target metric.** Design every post to be DM-able:
   "tag the friend who's in her reputation era", "send this to your
   clown-in-arms". Shares → Explore page → strangers.
4. **Keyword-rich captions** ("Instagram SEO"): captions and on-screen text
   carry searchable phrases ("taylor swift easter eggs", "eras tour
   surprise songs") — IG search is a real discovery surface in 2026.
5. **Collab posts** (IG's co-author feature) with mid-size fan accounts
   once we have something to offer — one collab puts us in front of their
   entire audience. Desk identifies candidates in the weekly rollup;
   outreach DMs are drafted by the desk, sent by a human.
6. **Post when Swifties scroll:** evenings US time + weekend mornings;
   let our own insights data override this the moment we have any.

**What "working" looks like weekly (brief rollup):** follower delta, reach,
shares per post, saves per carousel, profile-visit → follow rate, site
clicks (UTM). Kill formats that are flat for three weeks; double formats
that share.

**Daily pulse, separate from the above (added 2026-07-17, Joey's ask):** the
Founders' Brief now also carries a one-line daily Growth snapshot — follower
count + delta per platform, posts published today
(`.github/workflows/growth-snapshot.yml` → `docs/agents/marjorie.md` › Brief
format). It's a leading indicator only, deliberately lighter than the weekly
rollup above (no reach/shares/saves — that analysis stays weekly). Site
traffic joins it once Vercel Web Analytics ships (#799); app downloads/DAU
once iOS/Android ship (#67, #530).

## 7. Reddit & Tumblr — the etiquette lane (read before ANY promo post)

These communities convert best and punish promotion hardest. The recipe:

- **Be a member first.** 20-30 genuine, useful, zero-link contributions
  before any mention of the site. Account age matters; launch-day promo
  from a fresh account = removal + likely ban + a soured mod relationship.
- **When we do post the site:** transparent fan-made framing ("I'm a
  swiftie who builds things; I made a free era time-machine; no ads, no
  accounts; tear it apart"), posted by Joey's human-feeling account, ready
  to answer every comment personally for the first hours (drafted help
  from the desk, sent by human).
- **Check each sub's current self-promo rules in its sidebar first**, and
  when ambiguous, modmail *before* posting. (TX: Joey.)
- Tumblr: same spirit, softer rules — long-form theory posts native to the
  platform, tagged properly, with the site as a byline rather than a pitch.

## 8. Site ↔ social integration (Joey's holistic questions, answered)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| IG feed embedded on the site? | **No.** Heavy scripts + trackers + stale-feed risk vs. our static, fast, private site. Footer icons suffice. |
| FB on the site? | **No link needed.** The FB Page is Business-Suite infrastructure, not audience. Footer: IG, TikTok, X. |
| Social → site or site → social? | **Social → site** is the launch engine. Site → social exists only as footer icons + (later) a share-this-moment button, which turns delighted readers into distributors — the only site→social investment worth making. |
| How do we measure it? | Every bio/post link carries UTM tags; Vercel analytics splits traffic by source. Desk reports weekly. |

**Build-desk tickets to file (small, non-blocking, this week):** footer
social icons once handles exist; verify Open Graph share cards look right
when the site is pasted into IG DMs/X/Discord (this is how fans share it
peer-to-peer — it must look great); optional `/follow` page later.
