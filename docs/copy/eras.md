# Era mood/intro blurbs

1–2 sentences per era, shown as that era's description. Fan-editor voice,
third-person, per `docs/marketing/content-framework-2026-07-03.md` — not
first-person as Taylor, no lyric reproduction (see `unofficial.md`). Era
names/order/years match `supabase/seed/eras-data.mjs` exactly.

---

### The debut era
**2006–2008 · *Taylor Swift***
A teenager in Nashville turning crushes and heartbreak into hits, curly
hair and cowboy boots included — the start of the storytelling habit that
never really left.

### The Fearless era
**2008–2010 · *Fearless***
Sparkly dresses and fairy-tale imagery for the album that turned her into
a household name — the record that pushed country-pop all the way into
the mainstream, built almost entirely out of teenage heartbreak.

### The Speak Now era
**2010–2012 · *Speak Now***
Written solo, start to finish, in ballgowns and purple lighting — the
most defiantly personal record of her early career, with more than one
song people spent years trying to decode.

### The Red era
**2012–2014 · *Red***
Red lips, fall colors, and heartbreak stretched across every genre she
could reach — the record where "country artist" stopped being the whole
story.

### The 1989 era
**2014–2017 · *1989***
A full reinvention: New York City, Polaroid-style visuals, and a total
pivot to pop that reset what people expected from her next.

### The reputation era
**2017–2019 · *reputation***
Snakes, black and white, and a defiant answer to a brutal public
backlash cycle — the coldest, most armored era, worn on purpose.

### The Lover era
**2019–2020 · *Lover***
Pastel skies after reputation's black-and-white — the era that let
romance and softness be loud again, glitter hearts included.

### The folklore era
**2020 · *folklore***
A surprise pandemic album that went full cottagecore overnight —
cardigans, invented characters, and the quietest, most literary turn of
her catalog.

### The evermore era
**2020–2022 · *evermore***
folklore's sister record, pushed further into the same woods — flannel,
piano ballads, and more of the fictional storytelling that started next
door.

### The Midnights era
**2022–2024 · *Midnights***
Late-night, diaristic, moody blues and purples — a concept record about
restless nights that doubled as the run-up to the Eras Tour.

### The Tortured Poets era
**2024–2025 · *The Tortured Poets Department***
A bruised, word-heavy breakup record — sprawling and diaristic, with a
lot resting on what any given line might mean.
